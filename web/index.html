Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free! No hidden costs, no premium features, no ads. It's open-source and community-driven.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally on your device. Your data never leaves Google's servers.

Does it work on mobile?

Not yet. Desktop browsers only (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). Mobile support is planned for v1.2.

Can I use it with shared sheets?

Yes! If you have edit access to a sheet, you can sort it. Read-only sheets can't be modified.

How do I get support?

Check our documentation, visit FAQ, or open an issue on GitHub.

Can I contribute?

Yes! We welcome contributions. See our contributing guide on GitHub.
