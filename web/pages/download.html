Installation Guide

Chrome Extension (Recommended)

1

Click "Install Now"

You'll be taken to the Chrome Web Store. Click the "Add to Chrome" button.

2

Confirm Installation

Chrome will ask for permissions. Click "Add extension" to confirm.

3

Find Your Icon

Look for the sorting icon in your toolbar. Click it to start using!

Google Workspace Add-on

1

Open Google Sheets

Open any Google Sheet on sheets.google.com

2

Go to Extensions

Click on "Extensions" menu → "Add-ons" → "Get add-ons"

3

Search & Install

Search for "Google Sheets Sorting Helper" and click "Install"

4

Grant Permissions

Allow the add-on to access your Google Sheets

Installation FAQ

Do I need to pay?

No, it's completely free! No subscription, no ads, no hidden costs.

Is it safe?

Yes. The extension is open source and audited. Your data never leaves Google's servers.

What about privacy?

We only process your data locally on your device. No tracking without your consent.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, and Safari are all supported.

Can I use both?

Yes! Install both the extension and add-on. They work together nicely.
